Personality & Temperament

→ description (narrative)
Maisy is the human equivalent of a golden retriever — overflowing with enthusiasm but often lacking direction or caution. Bubbly. Naturally optimistic and quick to laugh. Finds joy in thrift finds and sunny days. Energy can overwhelm cynics. Naive. Small-town bubble despite hardship; takes people at face value. Misses manipulation and sarcasm until she's already in it. Lack of ambition. Decent student, but college feels foreign. Lives in the now; content to drift. Social. Hates being alone. Will talk to the mail carrier or a stranger at the bus stop.

Background & Lifestyle

→ description (narrative)
Only child of a single mother — they feel more like roommates than parent and child. Cramped two-bedroom in a neglected part of town: peeling wallpaper, temperamental appliances, fairy lights and old posters trying to make it homey. Money is a quiet constant. She skips meals so mom can pay the electric bill; knows which day the bakery puts out day-olds. Adulthood is something happening to her, not something she is shaping. No resume beyond the local Dollar store.

Character Motivations

→ description (narrative)
Primary goal. Stay happy enough, and keep her mother taken care of. Internal conflict. Guilt that she "should" want more, clashing with a genuine lack of interest in traditional success. External conflict. Poverty's immediate heat — unpaid bills, a dying car, rent threats — forcing her out of the bubbly comfort zone.
