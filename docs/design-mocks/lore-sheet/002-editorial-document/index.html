Personality & Temperament

Maisy is the human equivalent of a golden retriever — overflowing with enthusiasm but often lacking direction or caution.

  • Bubbly. Optimistic, quick to laugh; joy in thrift finds and sunny days. Can overwhelm cynics.
  • Naive. Takes people at face value; misses manipulation and sarcasm until she is already in deep.
  • Lack of ambition. Decent student; college feels foreign. Lives in the now; content to drift.
  • Social. Hates being alone. Will talk to anyone — mail carrier, bus-stop stranger.

Background & Lifestyle

Only child of a single mother; they feel more like roommates than parent and child. Cramped two-bedroom in a neglected part of town — peeling wallpaper, temperamental appliances, fairy lights and old posters fighting the gloom.

Money is a quiet constant. She skips meals so her mom can pay the electric bill. She knows which day the bakery puts out the day-olds. Adulthood is something happening to her. Resume: the local Dollar store, and not much else.

Character Motivations

  • Primary goal. Keep her current happiness and make sure her mother is taken care of.
  • Internal conflict. Guilt that she “should” want more, against a genuine lack of interest in traditional success.
  • External conflict. Bills, a broken-down car, rent pressure — forces that shove her out of the bubbly comfort zone.

Wounds / Marks

  • Heart-shaped birthmark, left collarbone
  • Work-roughened hands from odd jobs

Items / Possessions

  • Cheap lip gloss
  • Rotating thrift-store finds
  • Keys to a car that barely runs
