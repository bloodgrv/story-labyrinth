# Deep Writing Sessions Guide

A distraction-free mode for the Main Editor: hides every sidebar and menu, fills the screen with just your writing, and optionally tracks a timer or word-count goal — with a calmer color theme and a few gentle, entirely optional wellbeing check-ins for long stretches at the keyboard.

> [!NOTE]
> This is a bigger step than the toolbar's **Maximize Editor** button (the two-pane icon next to it), which just collapses the side panels for more room. A Writing Session goes further — hiding the top bar too, applying an adaptive theme, and optionally tracking a goal — and it's something you deliberately start and end, not a layout you toggle.

---

## 1. Starting a Session

_Path: click the target-like **Focus** icon in the Main Editor's toolbar (next to Maximize Editor), or press `Ctrl+Alt+F` (`⌘+Option+F` on Mac)_

In the dialog that opens, pick:

- **Goal** — Open-ended (no goal, just write), a **Timer** (15/25/45/60 minutes or your own value), or a **Word Count** target (250/500/1000/2000 words or your own value — only available with a chapter open, since it tracks words written _in that chapter_ from the moment you start).
- **Theme** — see below.
- Optional wellbeing check-ins — see below.

Click **Start Writing** and the sidebars, top bar, and editor tool panel all disappear. A small floating bar at the top of the screen is all that's left, showing elapsed time and (if you set one) your goal's progress.

Press `Ctrl+Alt+F` again, or click **End Session** in that floating bar, to leave at any time — your writing is saved exactly as it always is; a session doesn't change how or when your work is saved.

> [!TIP]
> Every option you pick is remembered for next time, so starting your next session can be as simple as clicking the icon and hitting **Start Writing** again.

## 2. Adaptive Themes

Four theme options, chosen independently of your normal light/dark preference (picking one here never changes the rest of the app):

| Theme        | What it looks like                                              |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Default      | Matches whatever your normal app theme already is               |
| Warm         | Warm sepia/amber tones — easier on the eyes for evening writing |
| Low Contrast | Softer, muted contrast to reduce eye strain over a long session |
| Night        | Very dim, deep tones for late-night writing in a dark room      |

You can change the theme from the Start Session dialog, or on the fly from the small selector in the floating bar — switching mid-session fades smoothly rather than snapping to the new colors.

## 3. Timers, Word Goals, and Progress

If you set a Timer or Word Count goal, the floating bar shows your progress at a glance (time remaining, or words written toward your target, with a small progress bar). Reaching your goal doesn't end the session or interrupt your typing — it shows a celebratory, dismissible check-in ("Time's up!" / "Word goal reached!") offering **Keep Writing** or **End Session**. The choice is always yours.

## 4. Wellbeing Check-Ins

All three are off by default — nothing appears unless you turn it on:

| Option                      | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Break reminders**         | A gentle "Time for a quick break?" nudge every N minutes (your choice), with **Snooze 10 min** or **Dismiss** — it keeps recurring for the rest of the session at your chosen interval                                                             |
| **Session length check-in** | A one-time "nice work" check-in once you hit a chosen session length, asking whether to keep going or wrap up — never forces you to stop                                                                                                           |
| **Content check-in**        | Adds a short, private, supportive line to the two reminders above — a nudge to check in with yourself if the material you're writing is emotionally heavy going. This is for your own wellbeing while writing, not a reader-facing content warning |

Every one of these is a dismissible suggestion, never a lock — closing a reminder or choosing "Keep Writing" always lets you continue exactly where you were.

## 5. Troubleshooting

| Problem                                 | What's happening                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Word Count goal option is disabled      | Open a chapter first — a word-count goal needs a chapter to count words in                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| The floating bar's word count looks off | It counts words written _since the session started_, in the chapter you started it on — switching to a different chapter mid-session keeps tracking the original one                                                                                         |
| Sidebars didn't come back after ending  | Make sure you clicked **End Session** (or pressed `Ctrl+Alt+F`) rather than just navigating away — switching to another tool (e.g. Lorebook) mid-session temporarily restores the chrome there too, but the session itself is still running until you end it |

---

Session settings and theme choice are stored locally on your device, alongside the rest of your workspace preferences — no account or cloud sync required.
