# The Absolute Basics

Welcome to Story Labyrinth! This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get started with your AI-powered writing journey.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Quick Start (5 Minutes)**
> 1. Go to **Settings → Providers & keys** → Add an API key (OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Local API URL)
> 2. Click **Stories** in the left sidebar → **Create New Story** → Enter title, author, language
> 3. Click **Start in Brainstorm** on the new story's card → talk through your idea (or use Guided setup) → Accept its synopsis proposal and Open any handoffs to Outline/World-Building/Notes
> 4. Back on the story dashboard, **Create New Chapter** → click **Write** → start typing, or open the Editor Chat to ask the AI to write or revise prose
> 5. That's it! Read the detailed guide below for advanced features.

## 1. Set Up Your AI Connection

Before you can use AI features, you'll need to connect to an AI provider. Story Labyrinth supports OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Grok (xAI, key or OAuth), or a local AI model.

_Path: **Settings** (bottom of the left sidebar) → **Providers & keys** for API keys, **Local** for a local connection, **Feature routing** to point individual features at specific providers_

| Provider | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **OpenRouter** | Enter your OpenRouter API key to access a variety of models from different providers. |
| **OpenAI** | Enter your OpenAI API key to access models like GPT-4 and GPT-3.5. |
| **DeepSeek** | Enter your DeepSeek API key to access DeepSeek's own models directly (deepseek-chat, deepseek-reasoner). |
| **Grok (xAI)** | Enter an API key, or connect via OAuth for a SuperGrok session — both live under Providers & keys. |
| **Local** | Connect to a locally hosted model by entering the API URL (default: `http://localhost:1234/v1`) under the **Local** settings tab. |

> [!TIP]
> After adding your API key, available models will be automatically retrieved. For local models, you'll need to click the "Refresh Models" button.

## 2. Understand Series vs Stories

Story Labyrinth supports two ways to organise your writing:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Standalone Stories** | Individual stories that aren't connected to others. Perfect for one-off projects, short stories, or independent novels. |
| **Series** | Collections of related stories that share a world, characters, or timeline. Ideal for sequels, prequels, or interconnected narratives. |

> [!TIP]
> Series are optional. You can create standalone stories directly, or group related stories into a series later. Stories in a series share lorebook entries automatically.

## 3. Create Your First Story

Once your AI connection is set up, you can create your first story.

1. From the Home page, choose either **Stories** (for standalone) or **Series** (to create a series first)
2. Click **Create New Story**, enter title, author, and language — the synopsis field can stay blank for now, Brainstorm can help you fill it in next
3. For series: pick your series from the "Series" dropdown in that same dialog (or leave it as "None" for a standalone story — you can assign it to a series later by editing the story)

_Path: **Stories** or **Series** in the left sidebar — or_ [go to Series directly](/series)

> [!TIP]
> Once a story is open, the Stories tab shows a **Close Active Story** button next to Import Story. It clears the current session and returns you to the story list — nothing is lost, since chapter content already autosaves as you type.

### Start in Brainstorm, not a blank chapter

A brand-new story's card offers a **Start in Brainstorm** shortcut — the recommended way in, instead of jumping straight to an empty Editor. Brainstorm is an intake/orientation chat: talk through your idea in your own words, or click **Guided setup** and pick Light/Standard/Grill-me depending on how much of an interview you want.

As the conversation develops your premise, genre, characters, and setting, Brainstorm proposes what it's learned back to you — never writing anything automatically:

- An **overview proposal** to fill in (or rewrite) your story's synopsis — **Accept** to apply it
- **Handoffs** to Outline, World-Building, or Notes — **Open** to jump to that desk with the content pre-seeded into its composer, ready to review and send

Both land in the **Approvals** panel and stay there until you **Mark done**, so nothing you talked through gets lost if you don't act on it right away. See the **Brainstorm Guide** for the full walkthrough, including the Setup checklist that tracks what's still missing (Premise, Genre & Tone, Protagonist(s), Setting, Central Conflict).

Once you're happy with where the story stands, click **Open Editor** on the story's card (or the card itself, for its Dashboard hub) and move on to writing.

## 4. Create Your First Chapter

Now that you have a story, it's time to create your first chapter.

1. From your story dashboard, click **Create New Chapter**
2. Enter a title for your chapter
3. Optionally, set a POV (Point of View) character and type
4. Click **Create** to save your new chapter
5. Click the **Write** button on the chapter card to open the editor

## 5. Write with AI Assistance

The Story Labyrinth editor allows you to write manually or with AI assistance through the **Editor Chat** and selection-based rework.

### Using the Editor Chat

The Editor Chat is a docked conversation alongside your chapter — it always has your chapter's text, Codex entries, and other story context available.

1. Open the Editor Chat panel next to your chapter
2. Describe what you want written (e.g., "Write a tense confrontation between the detective and suspect")
3. Select an AI model from your configured providers
4. Send the message and wait for the AI to reply (streaming will show progress)
5. Review the reply and click **Accept** to insert the prose into your chapter, or keep the conversation going to refine it first

### Reworking Existing Text

To revise text you've already written, select it and use the floating toolbar:

1. Highlight a passage in your chapter
2. Use **"Rework in chat"** to discuss the change in the Editor Chat, or the **"More"** menu for a quick Expand/Rewrite/Shorten
3. Accept the reply to replace just that selection

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Editor Tips**
> - Be specific in your instructions: Instead of "continue the story," try "describe the character's reaction to the surprising news, showing their inner conflict"
> - **@ Autocomplete:** Type `@` in the editor to trigger lorebook tag autocomplete. Select a tag to quickly reference lorebook entries
> - **Word Count:** Real-time word count displays automatically as you write

## What's Next?

These are just the basics to get you started. Story Labyrinth offers many more advanced features to enhance your writing experience:

- Create a **Lorebook** with hierarchical levels (global, series, story) to manage characters, locations, and other story elements
- Customise **Prompts** to tailor the AI's writing style and behaviour
- Come back to **Brainstorm** any time you're stuck, not just at the start — it's also where you can import a character bible document to seed several Lorebook entries at once
- Organise related stories into **Series** with shared world-building

Explore the other guides to learn more about these advanced features.
