# Playbook Packs

Playbook packs are interview curriculum documents you can arm onto a World-Building **Character** chat — coverage targets and sample question angles for that chat to draw on. They're a writing aid for shaping the interview itself, never story canon: nothing in a pack is written to your Codex or lorebook directly.

---

## 1. What a Pack Covers

Every pack targets one of three playbooks:

| Playbook | Feeds |
|----------|-------|
| **Character — Concrete Coverage** | The base Character interview (wardrobe, appearance, wounds, items, background) |
| **Character — Psych Module** | The opt-in psych interview (MBTI, Enneagram, motivation, speech patterns) |
| **Character — Sexuality Module** | The opt-in sexuality interview (orientation, dynamic, kinks, limits) |

Each pack also has a **style** — Light, Standard, Grill-me, or Any — matching the same style dropdown used by Guided Setup, so a pack can target a specific interview intensity or apply regardless of it.

## 2. Pack Scopes

| Scope | Visible from | Editable? |
|-------|--------------|-----------|
| **Shipped** | Every story | No — copy it to Global or This story first to customise |
| **Global** | Every story | Yes |
| **This story** | Just that story | Yes |

A story-scoped pack overrides a global one for the same playbook + style; global overrides shipped. Use the **copy icon** on a shipped pack to duplicate it into Global or This story, where it becomes a real editable pack.

## 3. Managing Packs

*Path: **Settings → Writing tools → Playbook Packs**, or from a Character WB chat's **Playbook** rail icon, click **Open Playbooks***

Settings' card has its own **Manage packs for** story picker, so you can review or edit story-scoped packs for any story without having that story open — the in-chat "Open Playbooks" link just lands here pre-scoped to whichever story you're in.

- **New Pack** — pick a Playbook and Style, give it a title, and write the body in markdown (coverage targets, sample questions). Saves at Global scope from Settings' global view, or This-story scope from an in-story context.
- **Edit** (pencil icon) — only Global/This-story packs; shipped packs must be copied first.
- **Delete** (trash icon) — only Global/This-story packs.
- **Import** — upload a `.md` or `.txt` file. An optional frontmatter block at the top of the file (`playbookKey:`, `style:`, `packScope:`, `title:`) sets those fields automatically; otherwise the dialog's own dropdowns are used.

## 4. Using a Pack in Chat

On a Character WB chat, the **Use playbook pack** toggle sits next to Guided Setup in the **Playbook** rail panel. Turning it on arms whichever pack matches the chat's current style and the module you're interviewing for (base/psych/sexuality) — the chat then follows that pack's coverage targets and sample questions instead of (or alongside) its own default framing.

> [!TIP]
> If no Global or This-story pack exists for a given playbook + style, the shipped default is used automatically — you never have to author a pack just to get an interview started.

---

Playbook packs are organisational/config content, not chat history — creating, editing, or deleting one doesn't touch any existing chat, Codex entry, or lorebook data.
